## Common patterns in his current apps (what to keep / what to drop)

Keep:
- **Huge cards and numbers.** His Solitaire uses oversized card indices; Blackjack cards fill the width.
- Simple bottom action bars with icon + word (Undo, Settings, Hint, New Game / Stand, Split, Double, Hit).
- Hint buttons everywhere. He keeps them on.
- Detailed stats and high-score tables (he has years of history; see per-game notes).
- He turns **sound off** in every game. Default sound off or make it obvious.

Drop (the "scam" parts):
- Banner ads on every screen, full-screen ads on open, fake "Skip" buttons that open the Play Store.
- Sweepstakes-casino ads (Chumba) inside a solitaire game.
- Event/quest/spin-wheel pop-up chains, "Remove Ads" upsells, coins with a green "+" (buy) button.
- Locked modes behind updates ("Journey", Woodoku).
